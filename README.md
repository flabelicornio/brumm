# Brumm — Music Player

Free audio and video player for desktop, browser, and mobile. Runs locally, without external dependencies (supports streaming music and videos from public online repositories).

Opens folders and removable media.

## Supported formats
Audio:
MP3, FLAC, AAC, M4A, OGG, WAV, OPUS, WMA

Video:
MP4, WebM, MKV, MOV, AVI, M4V, OGV, 3GP

Important note: Brumm supporting a format does not guarantee your device can play it—that depends on system codecs. Formats that always work in browsers are MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, and OGG for audio, and MP4 (H.264) and WebM (VP8/VP9) for video. MKV, AVI, and WMA support depends on the specific content.
